Principal Investigators & Collaborators

A national collaborative alliance spanning IIT Delhi, IISc Bengaluru, IISER Mohali, IIT Bhubaneswar, IIT Palakkad, and global technology innovators NVIDIA, Microsoft Research, and EyeClimate.

Lead Principal Investigator (PI)

Dr. Sarvesh Kumar Dubey

Associate Professor • Centre for Atmospheric Sciences (CAS)

Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (Lead Host Institution)

Dr. Dubey leads the scientific architecture of PRITHVI, bridging large-scale atmospheric modeling, geodesic dynamical cores, and physics-informed neural network frameworks. Coordinates the integration of Microsoft Aurora with Wavelet-PINN to eliminate dynamical drift over the Indian domain.

skdubey@cas.iitd.ac.in
