Tier 3: 4 Work Packages Detailed Institutional Objectives & Deliverables
WP 1 Lead: IISc (CDS), IITD (CAS)

Physics-Aware Regional Foundation Model Development

  • Couple Aurora data-driven emulator with Wavelet-PINN (W-PINN).
  • Train using Indian datasets (IMDAA, IMD, TRMM/GPM, Mission Mausam).
  • Incorporate two coupling strategies: fine-tuned hybrid and integrated PINN adaptation.
Output: Physics-aware regional foundational model prototype (open v1.0).
WP 2 Lead: IITD (CAS), IISc (CDS)

Model Evaluation & Multiscale Diagnostics

  • Evaluate model skill for rainfall, winds, temperature, and surface fluxes.
  • Benchmark Aurora-W-PINN against GraphCast, IFS-HRES, and operational IMD forecasts.
  • Conduct uncertainty and drift analysis using ensemble and long-lead experiments.
Output: Quantified accuracy, stability, and physical realism for Indian conditions.
WP 3 Lead: IISER Mohali, IIT Palakkad

Sectoral Downstream Applications

  • Himalayan rainfall extremes & hybrid AI flash-flood early warning.
  • Streamflow & reservoir operation forecasts for the Western Ghats.
  • Fog & air quality in Indo-Gangetic Plains; solar/wind predictions for grid management.
Output: Demonstrated multi-sectoral utility for disaster, water, and energy planning.
WP 4 Lead: IITD, IISER Mohali, IIT BB

Dissemination, Dashboard & Policy Assessment

  • Build visualization and forecast dashboards for public and policy interfaces.
  • Develop reproducible training pipelines, data APIs, and open-source releases.
  • Assess societal and economic benefits under Mission Mausam’s AI-enabled services.
Output: Operational readiness of an open, physics-aware AI platform for India.
