Stage 03 4 Coordinated Work Packages (WP1 to WP4)
WP1 Lead: IIT Delhi • IISc

Physics-Aware Foundation Model

  • Fine-tuning Strategy: Multi-resolution adaptation of Microsoft Aurora backbone on IMDAA/ERA5.
  • Physics Constraints: Continuous Navier-Stokes wavelet derivatives for mass & momentum conservation.
  • Mesh Scaling: Adaptation to ~0.1° (~10 km) regional grid over the Indian domain.
  • Loss Balancing: Dynamic weighting between data-driven RMSE and physical PDE residual losses.
Key Deliverables Trained Checkpoint v1.0 • Open Weights Repository
WP2 Lead: IIT Delhi • IISER

Multi-Scale Skill Evaluation

  • Validation Benchmarking: Multi-year verification against IMD radar and rain gauge observations.
  • Spectral Diagnostics: Wavelet kinetic energy spectrum analysis across synoptic & convective scales.
  • Drift Analysis: Auditing error accumulation and conservation stability for 1–15 day rollouts.
  • Extreme Event Auditing: Verification during cloudbursts, western disturbances, and depressions.
Key Deliverables Evaluation Benchmark Suite • Comprehensive Performance Report
WP3 Lead: IITBBS • IITPKD • IITD

Multi-Sectoral Impact Engines

  • Himalayan Extremes: High-resolution early warnings for flash floods and landslide hazards.
  • Hydrology & Dams: Streamflow routing and reservoir inflow modeling using WRF-Hydro integration.
  • Clean Energy: Direct normal irradiance (DNI) and wind field predictions for renewable power grids.
  • Air Quality & Fog: Boundary layer forecasting for Indo-Gangetic fog and particulate transport.
Key Deliverables Sectoral Decision Support Toolkits • Specialized Deployment APIs
WP4 Lead: IIT Delhi • NVIDIA

Dissemination & National Platform

  • Open Model Hub: Public release of model weights, inference scripts, and training recipes.
  • Operational Serving: Containerized deployment on IndiaAI GPU clusters for semi-operational trials.
  • Community Engagement: National hackathons, workshops, and comprehensive user guides.
  • Policy Integration: Mission Mausam interoperability and alignment with national meteorological services.
Key Deliverables FAIR-Compliant Open Platform • Operational Documentation
