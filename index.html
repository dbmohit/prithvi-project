Theoretical Motivation & Innovation

The Pinnora Scientific Architecture

Overcoming the fundamental thermodynamic and dynamical limitations of purely data-driven black-box neural networks over the Indian tropical monsoon terrain.

Limitation

Pure Data-Driven Models

Global foundation models (Aurora, GraphCast, Pangu-Weather) trained on ERA5 reanalysis suffer from dynamical drift, non-conservation of moisture and total energy, and hallucinate unphysical extreme precipitation over complex Indian topography.

The Core Engine

Pinnora (PINN + Aurora)

Coupling Microsoft Aurora's 3D Swin-Transformer backbone with Wavelet-PINNs (W-PINNs). Analytical wavelet derivatives enforce Navier-Stokes continuous PDEs without automatic differentiation memory overheads.

Guaranteed Fidelity

Physics-Bounded Prediction

Guarantees strict conservation of mass, momentum, and moisture. Delivers 2× to 3× faster training convergence, stable multi-week lead times, and seamless downscaling with IMD/IMDAA radar and satellite datasets.

National Preparedness & Services

Downstream Sectoral Impact Domains

PRITHVI directly empowers critical national sectors aligned with Mission Mausam priorities through physics-consistent regional forecasts.

01

Himalayan Rainfall & Floods

Lead: Dr. Raju Attada • IISER Mohali

Early warning of extreme precipitation, cloudburst triggers, and debris flow susceptibility across Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and Jammu & Kashmir with high-resolution topographic boundary conditions.

02

Hydrology & Dam Operations

Lead: Dr. Subhasis Mitra • IIT Palakkad

Coupling Pinnora precipitation outputs with WRF-Hydro routing to forecast river discharges, reservoir inflows, and urban water availability across vulnerable peninsular and Himalayan basins.

03

Winter Fog & Air Quality

Lead: Dr. Ravi Kunchala • CAS, IIT Delhi

Predicting boundary-layer inversions, aerosol-fog feedback mechanisms, and particulate dispersion in the Indo-Gangetic Plains to aid aviation, railway transit, and urban health authorities.

04

Solar & Renewable Energy

Lead: Prof. Ravi Kumar K • DESE, IIT Delhi

High-fidelity solar irradiance (GHI, DNI) and boundary-layer wind field forecasting for regional power dispatch centers, battery storage schedules, and national renewable grid balancing.

05

Tropical Cyclone Dynamics

Lead: Dr. Abhishek Savita • CAS, IIT Delhi

Leveraging spectral scale energetics and vortex-scale asymmetry diagnostics to predict sudden track deviations and rapid intensity escalation in Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea cyclones, extending lead times by 48–72 hours.

06

Generative Downscaling

Collaboration: NVIDIA Earth-2 • CorrDiff

Deploying diffusion-based super-resolution models to downscale foundation model outputs to sub-kilometer urban hazard grids, generating probabilistic ensembles for local municipal disaster response.

Phase-Wise Technology Readiness Levels (TRL)

Year 1 • Phase I (TRL 3 → 4)

Aurora–W-PINN Hybrid Prototype

Integration of physics-aware loss modules; deployment of high-performance GPU training infrastructure; baseline benchmarking against raw Aurora.

Year 2 • Phase II (TRL 5 → 6)

Regional High-Resolution Model (0.1°)

Multi-scale verification using IMD radar and gridded precipitation; deployment of LoRA-adapted sectoral downstream modules.

Year 3 • Phase III (TRL 7 → 8)

Operational National Platform

Demonstration in semi-operational and real-time settings; integration into IMD/MoES Mission Mausam decision support services.

Computational Backbone

HPC & National AI Infrastructure

PRITHVI leverages India’s premier computational infrastructure across the IndiaAI Compute Platform and the National Supercomputing Mission (NSM).

IndiaAI GPU Platform

Dedicated to fine-tuning Microsoft Aurora, LoRA parameter-efficient adaptation, physics-aware W-PINN sweeps, and ensemble rollouts.

  • Total Compute Allocation: 900,000 A100 / H100 GPU-Hours
  • NVMe Scratch Storage: 120 TB High-Throughput NVMe
  • Workload Scope: 150+ PINN Sweeps • Sectoral Fine-Tuning

National Supercomputing Mission (NSM)

Generates high-fidelity ground-truth training simulations: 0.05° WRF reforecasts, WRF-Solar radiative simulations, and WRF-Hydro routing meshes.

  • Total Compute Allocation: 7,000,000 CPU Core-Hours
  • HPC Archive Storage: 120 TB Long-Term Simulation Store
  • Target Facilities: NSM Supercomputing Nodes • IIT Delhi HPC
