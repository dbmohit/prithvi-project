Scientific Motivation

Why Physics-Informed Regional AI?

Global atmospheric foundation models represent a quantum leap in weather forecasting speed, but lack physical law enforcement over complex tropical and orographic domains like the Indian monsoon.

⚠️

Pure Data-Driven Models

Models like Aurora, GraphCast, and Pangu-Weather are trained on global reanalysis (ERA5). Over India, they experience dynamical drift, non-conservation of moisture/energy, and hallucinate unphysical extremes during the monsoon.

The Breakthrough
Pinnora (PINN + Aurora)

Coupling Microsoft Aurora's 3D Swin-Transformer backbone with Wavelet-PINNs (W-PINNs). Analytical wavelet derivatives enforce Navier-Stokes continuous PDEs without automatic differentiation memory overheads.

🛡️

Physics-Guaranteed Fidelity

Guarantees strict conservation of mass, momentum, and moisture. Delivers 2× to 3× faster training convergence, stable multi-week lead times, and seamless downscaling with IMD/IMDAA radar and satellite datasets.

National Resilience

High-Resolution Sectoral Applications

PRITHVI is engineered to directly serve India's socio-economic priorities under Mission Mausam, transforming high-resolution atmospheric physics into action.

🏔️

Himalayan Rainfall Extremes & Cloudbursts

Lead: IISER Mohali • IIT Delhi (CAS)

High-resolution ensemble downscaling (~0.1° to convection-permitting nests) to detect orographic lifting, moisture convergence, and rapid localized precipitation triggers before catastrophic flash floods occur.

🌊

Hydrology & Smart Reservoir Management

Lead: IIT Palakkad • IIT Delhi (CAS)

Coupling Pinnora precipitation outputs with WRF-Hydro routing models across Western Ghats river basins. Achieves Nash-Sutcliffe Efficiency (NSE) > 0.8 for proactive reservoir inflow forecasting and flood prevention.

🌫️

Fog & Urban Air Quality (AQI)

Lead: IIT Delhi (Centre for Atmospheric Sciences)

Physics-constrained boundary layer and aerosol-radiation parameterization tailored for the Indo-Gangetic Plains. Delivers winter fog formation forecasts and multi-day hazardous particulate guidance (POD > 0.85).

☀️

Renewable Solar & Wind Grid Integration

Lead: IIT Delhi (DESE) • Prof. Ravi Kumar K

Physics-aware solar irradiance (WRF-Solar) and planetary boundary-layer wind field forecasting. Reduces Mean Absolute Error (MAE) under 10% for grid balancing and India’s decarbonization targets.

🌀

Tropical Cyclones & Rapid Intensification (RI)

Lead: IIT Delhi (CAS) • Dr. Sarvesh Kumar Dubey

Leveraging spectral scale energetics and vortex-scale asymmetry diagnostics to predict sudden track deviations and rapid intensity escalation in Bay of Bengal cyclones, extending early-warning lead times by 48–72 hours.

📡

Interactive Public & Policy Dashboards

Lead: IIT Delhi • IIT Bhubaneswar • IISER Mohali

Open APIs, real-time spatial visualizers, and FAIR-compliant data distribution pipelines integrated into national meteorological and disaster-management infrastructure (NDMA, IMD, Mission Mausam).

Quantitative Targets

Technical Efficacy Benchmarks

PRITHVI establishes measurable performance milestones across regional forecast accuracy, dynamical lead times, and sectoral decision thresholds.

Scientific Domain Target Metric Expected Impact Outcome
Regional Forecast Skill ≥ 20% RMSE improvement over Aurora baseline Significantly superior prediction of Indian summer monsoon, mesoscale convective systems, and depressions.
Lead Time & Stability +2 to 3 days usable lead time; reduced drift Elimination of systematic dynamical drift; reliable early warnings for extreme convective events.
Multiscale Representation Enhanced Wavelet fidelity across scales Precise physical depiction of convective scales without spatial blurs typical of purely data-driven models.
Hydrology Sector Nash-Sutcliffe Efficiency (NSE) > 0.8 Accurate streamflow and proactive reservoir operations for flood mitigation in vulnerable river basins.
Solar Energy Sector Mean Absolute Error (MAE) < 10% High-confidence solar irradiance forecasts for regional power grid operators and dispatch scheduling.
Air Quality & Fog Probability of Detection (POD) > 0.85 Robust winter fog guidance for aviation and railway networks, and Indo-Gangetic pollution alerts.
Computational Efficiency 2× to 3× faster convergence ~70% to 80% reduction in GPU training cost compared to training foundation models from scratch.
Open-Science Interoperability Open Model Weights, Code, & APIs FAIR principles compliant (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) national open-science ecosystem.

Phase-Wise Technology Readiness Levels (TRL)

Year 1 • Phase I (TRL 3 → 4)

Aurora–W-PINN Hybrid Prototype

Integration of physics-aware loss modules; deployment of high-performance GPU training infrastructure; baseline benchmarking against raw Aurora.

Year 2 • Phase II (TRL 5 → 6)

High-Resolution Regional Model (≤ 0.1°)

Large-scale fine-tuning on IMDAA, IMD, INSAT, and Mission Mausam datasets; rigorous validation for monsoon rainfall, air quality, and cyclogenesis.

Year 3 • Phase III (TRL 7 → 8)

Open-Science Release & Sectoral Deployment

Public release of trained model weights, Python APIs, and live visualization dashboards; deployment across hydrology, energy, and disaster agencies.

High Performance Computing

HPC & National AI Compute Architecture

PRITHVI leverages India’s premier computational infrastructure across the IndiaAI Compute Platform and the National Supercomputing Mission (NSM).

IndiaAI GPU Platform (₹9.00 Crore)

Dedicated to fine-tuning Microsoft Aurora, LoRA rollouts, physics-aware W-PINN sweeps, multi-resolution regional downscaling, and multi-model ensemble rollouts.

  • Total Compute Hours: 900,000 A100 / H100 GPU-Hours
  • GPU-Side High-Speed Storage: 120 TB NVMe Cluster
  • Workload Tasks: 150+ PINN runs • Sectoral Integrations

National Supercomputing Mission (₹3.80 Cr)

Generates high-fidelity ground-truth training simulations: 0.05° WRF reforecasts, WRF-Solar radiative models, WRF-Hydro routing, and 0.01° extreme event nests.

  • Total Core Hours: 7,000,000 CPU Core-Hours
  • HPC Storage Capacity: 120 TB NSM HPC Archive
  • High-Performance Clusters: NSM Supercomputers • IIT Delhi HPC
